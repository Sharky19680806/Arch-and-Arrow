The process

How a house comes together.

  1. 01

    A first conversation

    Send a few details through the form or give us a call. We talk about the house, how you live in it, and how you would like it to feel at the end of a long day.

  2. 02

    Time in the house

    We visit, measure and look closely — at where the light falls, which floors and fittings should stay, and what the house has been asking for.

  3. 03

    The design & the sample board

    Plans, a palette and a board of real materials — limewash, wood, linen, velvet, brass — to hold up in the light of your own rooms before anything is ordered.

  4. 04

    Making & settling in

    The work is carried out, the pieces arrive and the rooms are put together — then we step back and let the house settle into itself.

The sample board

What the house is made of.

Limewash
Walls. Mineral, breathable, and never quite one colour — it moves with the light all day.
Heart pine
Floors, tables and beds. Dense, amber-toned wood that only gets warmer underfoot.
Belgian linen
Curtains, bedding and slipcovers. Relaxed, a little crumpled, better with every wash.
Cotton velvet
Upholstery. A deep pile that holds the evening light.
Unlacquered brass
Hardware, taps and lamps. Left bare, so it darkens where it is touched most.
